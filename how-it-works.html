SIMPLE BY DESIGN

Move files without the middleman.

Droply connects two browsers directly. Pick a file, share a short peer code, and download it on the other device.

Back to transfer
01 / SEND

Choose your files

Drag files into the Send Files panel or browse from your device. Files stay in your browser until you choose to share them.

02 / CONNECT

Share the peer code

Droply creates a six-character PeerJS ID. Share it directly or open the generated QR code on the receiving device.

03 / RECEIVE

Connect and choose

The receiver enters the code and gets a live list of available files. Each file can be downloaded separately.

04 / TRANSFER

Download directly

File data moves through an encrypted WebRTC data channel in chunks. The browser rebuilds the original file and verifies its contents before download.

WHAT YOU NEED

Two browsers, one open session

Both people keep Droply open while transferring. An internet connection is needed for PeerJS discovery; the file data then travels directly between the connected browsers.
